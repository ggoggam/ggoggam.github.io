It has been almost two years since ChatGPT was released. I had been following natural language processing research before it came out.
This post introduces reinforcement learning with human feedback (RLHF), the main approach used to train ChatGPT and competitors such as Claude and Gemini to respond in ways people prefer.
My recent research studies a new RLHF method that learns from previously collected feedback, so I have been spending a lot of time with the subject.
